Purpose

The Product Foundation defines the strategic structure of PantryPal. It establishes the principles that guide feature planning, prioritization, product evolution, and long-term consistency.

Product Definition

PantryPal is an AI-powered Kitchen Operating System designed to help users manage their kitchen through intelligent inventory management, contextual recommendations, and spatially familiar experiences.

Product Objectives
Reduce food waste.
Simplify kitchen management.
Enable smarter cooking decisions.
Create calm, intuitive experiences.
Build a scalable product ecosystem.
