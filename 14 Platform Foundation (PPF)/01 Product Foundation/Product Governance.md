Governance Philosophy

Every feature must align with:

Vision
Core Principles
Platform Foundation
Product Foundation
Approval Criteria

Before implementation, every feature must answer:

Does it solve a real kitchen problem?
Does it support PantryPal's philosophy?
Can it be maintained?
Does it reuse existing components?
Does it fit within the current version scope?

If any answer is "No", the feature should be revised or deferred.

Scope Management

No new features may be added to PantryPal v1.0 after the scope freeze unless they are critical bug fixes or essential architectural requirements.
