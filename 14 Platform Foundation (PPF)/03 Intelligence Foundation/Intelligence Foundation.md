Purpose

The Intelligence Foundation defines how PantryPal acquires, processes, interprets, and delivers intelligence across the platform.

It ensures that every AI-driven feature follows a unified architectural model rather than implementing isolated intelligence solutions.

Intelligence Mission

Transform kitchen data into meaningful, contextual decisions that help users without overwhelming them.

Core Intelligence Components
Kitchen Intelligence Core (KIC)
Kitchen Ready Score (KRS)
Kitchen Maturity Index (KMI)
Recommendation Engine
Inventory Intelligence Engine
AI Recognition Pipeline
Learning Engine
Context Engine
Notification Intelligence
Principles
Intelligence should remain invisible until needed.
Intelligence supports users; it does not replace them.
Recommendations must always be explainable.
Intelligence modules remain reusable across experiences.
