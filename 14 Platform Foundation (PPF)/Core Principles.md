These principles govern every decision made within PantryPal.

Principle 1

Kitchen First

Technology should enhance the kitchen rather than replace it.

Principle 2

Invisible Intelligence

AI should explain outcomes instead of exposing unnecessary complexity.

Principle 3

Progressive Disclosure

Information appears only when users need it.

Principle 4

Consistency Over Novelty

Users should build confidence through predictable interactions.

Principle 5

Spatial Memory

Objects remain where users naturally expect them.

Principle 6

Platform Thinking

Every new feature should contribute to the overall platform rather than exist as an isolated solution.

Principle 7

Evergreen Evolution

The platform should grow continuously without losing its identity.
