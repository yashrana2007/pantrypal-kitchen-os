# 🌿 PantryPal

## Project Evergreen

### Vision

To become the world's most trusted and intuitive Kitchen Operating System by helping every household organize food, reduce waste, eat healthier, and shop smarter.

PantryPal exists to simplify everyday kitchen life through thoughtful design, intelligent recommendations, and a deeply human user experience.

We believe technology should quietly support people rather than demand their attention.

Our long-term vision is to create a digital kitchen companion that feels as natural as opening your refrigerator.
