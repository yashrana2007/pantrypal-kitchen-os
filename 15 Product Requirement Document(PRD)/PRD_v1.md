Document Purpose

This Product Requirements Document (PRD) serves as the master specification for PantryPal v1.0.

It consolidates the product vision, strategic objectives, functional and non-functional requirements, user experience standards, release planning, and implementation guidance into a single authoritative document.

The PRD should be used alongside the Product Bible, Platform Foundation, Design System, and Engineering Documentation to guide product design, development, testing, and release.

Product Overview

PantryPal is an AI-powered Kitchen Operating System designed to simplify kitchen management through intelligent inventory tracking, meal planning, shopping assistance, analytics, and personalization.

Unlike traditional grocery or recipe applications, PantryPal acts as an intelligence layer that continuously understands the kitchen and assists users in making informed everyday decisions.

Product Mission

Create the world's most intelligent, human-centered Kitchen Operating System that helps every household make smarter kitchen decisions through calm, explainable, and context-aware AI.
