Section: Product Requirements

1. Purpose

This section defines the product requirements, business objectives, functional scope, priorities, and release boundaries for PantryPal v1.0.

It establishes the minimum product capabilities required before launch and provides the baseline against which implementation and acceptance will be measured.

2. Product Goals

PantryPal v1.0 is designed to achieve the following goals:

Goal 1

Reduce household food waste.

Goal 2

Simplify grocery planning.

Goal 3

Improve kitchen organization.

Goal 4

Support healthier cooking decisions.

Goal 5

Reduce cognitive effort during everyday kitchen management.

Goal 6

Create a scalable Kitchen Operating System foundation.

3. Business Objectives

PantryPal aims to:

Increase daily engagement.
Build long-term user trust.
Encourage habit formation.
Differentiate through AI-powered kitchen intelligence.
Establish a reusable platform for future versions.

5. Product Requirements

PantryPal shall:

Maintain an accurate inventory of kitchen items.
Recommend meals based on available ingredients.
Generate intelligent shopping recommendations.
Monitor kitchen health through analytics.
Personalize recommendations according to user preferences.
Provide clear explanations for AI-generated suggestions.
Support manual user control over all recommendations.

7. Acceptance Criteria

PantryPal v1.0 is considered feature-complete when:

All six core experiences are implemented.
Kitchen Intelligence Core supports every experience.
Product Bible aligns with implementation.
Design System compliance is verified.
Accessibility standards are satisfied.
Performance targets are met.
Security review is completed.
8. Dependencies

The Product Requirements depend on:

Platform Foundation (PPF)
Product Bible
Design System
Component Registry (PCR)
Engineering Architecture
9. Constraints

Development must:

Follow Platform Foundation principles.
Reuse PCR components.
Respect the v1.0 scope freeze.
Avoid introducing undocumented features.
Maintain backward compatibility within the v1.x release line where practical.
10. Success Criteria

The Product Requirements are considered fulfilled when PantryPal enables users to:

Manage kitchen inventory.
Plan meals confidently.
Shop intelligently.
Monitor kitchen performance.
Personalize their experience.
Complete everyday kitchen workflows from one unified platform.
