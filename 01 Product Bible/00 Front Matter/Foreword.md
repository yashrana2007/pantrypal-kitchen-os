Foreword

Project Evergreen is the first step toward PantryPal's long-term vision.

It represents our commitment to thoughtful product design, sustainable living, and human-centered technology.

Rather than building another kitchen management application, Project Evergreen explores a different question:

How can technology quietly improve everyday kitchen life?

Every chapter in this Product Bible exists to answer that question.

This document is not simply a design specification.

It is a record of the philosophy, principles, and decisions that shape PantryPal.
