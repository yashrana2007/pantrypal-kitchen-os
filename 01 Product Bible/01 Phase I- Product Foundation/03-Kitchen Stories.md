Project Evergreen

Why Kitchen Stories?

Behind every kitchen is a different lifestyle.

PantryPal isn't built for "users."

It's built for people.

Every feature, animation, interaction, and recommendation should make someone's everyday kitchen life a little easier.

These stories remind us who we're designing for.

🏡 Kitchen Story 01 — Anita Sharma
The Family Planner

Age: 42

Occupation: Marketing Manager

Family: Husband + 2 Children

Story

Sunday morning is Anita's planning day.

She opens the refrigerator.

Half a bottle of milk.

Three tomatoes.

One onion.

Some spinach.

She isn't sure what else is left.

Before PantryPal, she'd walk around the kitchen checking every shelf, then open three grocery apps to compare prices.

Now she opens PantryPal.

Within seconds she sees:

Your kitchen is 86% ready for the week.

The app tells her:

Tomatoes should be used today.
Milk will finish tomorrow.
Buying from BigBasket today saves ₹124.
Three dinners can be prepared using existing ingredients.

Planning the week now takes minutes instead of nearly an hour.

Anita's Goals
Save time
Reduce food waste
Plan groceries efficiently
Feed her family healthier meals
Stay within budget

💪 Kitchen Story 02 — Neha Kapoor
The Fitness Enthusiast

Age: 26

Occupation: Software Engineer

Story

Neha goes to the gym every evening.

Her biggest challenge isn't working out.

It's consistently eating enough protein while avoiding unnecessary grocery trips.

PantryPal quietly keeps track of her kitchen.

When paneer is running low, it's already in the Smart Cart.

When she's 24g short of her protein goal, the app suggests meals using ingredients already in her fridge.

She spends less time calculating and more time focusing on her fitness.

Goals
High-protein meals
Meal planning
Nutrition tracking
Budget-friendly shopping
