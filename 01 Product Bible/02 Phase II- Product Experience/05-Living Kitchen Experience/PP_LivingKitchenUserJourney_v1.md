1. Purpose

The Living Kitchen User Journey defines how users interact with PantryPal's intelligent kitchen ecosystem throughout their daily routine.

Unlike Smart Add, which focuses on adding inventory, Living Kitchen focuses on helping users understand, maintain, and improve their kitchen through proactive insights and contextual recommendations.

This journey demonstrates how PantryPal transforms passive pantry management into an intelligent daily experience.

2. Experience Philosophy

"Users shouldn't have to inspect their kitchen. Their kitchen should explain itself."

Living Kitchen continuously surfaces the most relevant information based on the current state of the pantry and the user's habits.

3. Primary User Goal

The user wants to understand:

What can I cook?
What needs attention?
What should I buy?
Is my kitchen healthy?
What changed today?

without manually searching through inventory.

6. Kitchen States Throughout the Journey

The Living Kitchen adapts according to the detected kitchen condition.

Healthy Kitchen

Primary message:

"You're well prepared today."

Suggested action:

Discover recipes.

Attention Needed

Primary message:

"A few ingredients need your attention."

Suggested action:

Use expiring ingredients.

Critical Kitchen

Primary message:

"Your kitchen needs restocking."

Suggested action:

Review shopping recommendations.

Empty Kitchen

Primary message:

"Let's build your kitchen."

Suggested action:

Start Smart Add.

9. Pain Points Addressed

Traditional pantry apps require users to:

Search inventory manually.
Check expiry dates individually.
Decide what to cook without guidance.
Build shopping lists from memory.

Living Kitchen removes these burdens through proactive intelligence.

10. Success Criteria

The journey is successful when users can answer the following within a few seconds:

What should I cook?
What needs attention?
What should I buy?
How healthy is my kitchen?
What changed today?

without navigating multiple screens.
