1. Purpose

The Living Kitchen Journey Matrix documents how PantryPal adapts the user experience according to the current state of the user's kitchen.

Unlike traditional pantry applications that present static inventory, the Living Kitchen dynamically adjusts its recommendations, priorities, messaging, and actions based on the kitchen's overall condition.

This matrix defines those adaptive behaviors.

2. Philosophy

"The experience should adapt to the kitchen—not force the user to adapt to the interface."

Every interaction should begin with understanding the kitchen's current state.

13. Evergreen Principle

"A Living Kitchen should never present the same experience twice—because no two kitchens are ever in exactly the same state."

The Living Kitchen continuously adapts to the user's inventory, habits, and kitchen condition, making every interaction timely and meaningful.
