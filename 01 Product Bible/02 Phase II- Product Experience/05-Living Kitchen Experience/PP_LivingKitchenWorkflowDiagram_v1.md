1. Purpose

The Living Kitchen Workflow Diagram defines the continuous intelligence cycle that powers PantryPal's Living Kitchen.

Unlike task-based workflows, the Living Kitchen operates as an always-evolving intelligence system that continuously analyzes the kitchen, generates insights, and learns from user behavior.

This document illustrates how information flows through PantryPal to maintain an up-to-date digital representation of the user's kitchen.

2. Philosophy

"A Living Kitchen never waits for the user—it continuously prepares to help them."

The workflow is event-driven rather than screen-driven.

Every inventory change, cooking activity, or shopping action contributes to a continuous intelligence cycle.

Layer 2 — Inventory Layer

Processes:

Storage Validation

Purpose:

Maintain an accurate kitchen inventory.

Layer 3 — Intelligence Layer

Powered by:

AI Confidence Scoring

Purpose:

Transform inventory into actionable intelligence.

Layer 4 — Recommendation Layer

Produces:

Meal Suggestions
Expiry Alerts
Shopping Recommendations
Inventory Priorities

Purpose:

Provide context-aware guidance.

Layer 5 — Experience Layer

Displayed to the user:

Living Kitchen Dashboard
Kitchen State
AI Insights
Suggested Actions

Purpose:

Deliver intelligence through a simple, understandable interface.

Layer 6 — Learning Layer

Learns from:

Accepted recommendations
Ignored recommendations

Shopping habits
Inventory corrections

Purpose:

Continuously personalize the Living Kitchen.
