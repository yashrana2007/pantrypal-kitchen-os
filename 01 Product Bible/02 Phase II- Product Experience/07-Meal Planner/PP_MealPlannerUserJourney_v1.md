1. Purpose

The Meal Planner User Journey illustrates how PantryPal transforms kitchen intelligence into practical cooking decisions by guiding users from meal discovery to meal preparation while minimizing food waste and reducing planning effort.

Unlike traditional recipe applications, PantryPal begins with inventory intelligence instead of recipe search.

2. Journey Objective

Help users confidently decide what to cook using available ingredients while maintaining nutritional balance and reducing unnecessary shopping.

3. User Persona
Primary Persona

Health-conscious Professional

Goals

Eat healthier
Save time
Reduce food waste
Cook using available ingredients
Plan meals efficiently

PantryPal Intelligence
Inventory Analysis
