1. Experience Overview

The Meal Planner Experience transforms kitchen intelligence into personalized cooking recommendations.

Instead of requiring users to search for recipes, PantryPal continuously evaluates inventory, ingredient freshness, nutritional information, cooking preferences, and kitchen readiness to recommend meals that are practical, relevant, and achievable.

The experience encourages users to cook with existing ingredients before suggesting additional purchases, reinforcing PantryPal's mission of reducing waste and simplifying kitchen management.

2. Problem Statement

Traditional recipe and meal planning applications often require users to manually search for recipes without considering the ingredients already available in their kitchen.

As a result, users frequently:

Purchase unnecessary ingredients.
Waste food already available at home.
Spend excessive time deciding what to cook.
Fail to utilize ingredients nearing expiry.
Create disconnected shopping and cooking workflows.

These challenges increase food waste, shopping costs, and daily decision fatigue.

3. Experience Vision

The Meal Planner should feel like an intelligent kitchen companion that understands the current state of the kitchen and recommends the best possible meals before the user begins searching.

Instead of asking:

"What recipe are you looking for?"

PantryPal asks:

"Based on your kitchen, here are the best meals you can cook today."

10. Design Principles
Kitchen First

Meals originate from inventory intelligence rather than recipe browsing.

Explainable AI

Every recommendation includes a clear reason.

Example:

Recommended because you have fresh spinach, mushrooms, and paneer that should be used soon.

Waste Reduction

Meals that utilize ingredients nearing expiry receive higher priority.

User Control

Users can browse, filter, save, and modify recommendations.

Consistency

The Meal Planner follows the PantryPal Platform Foundation, Design System, Component Registry, and Interaction Principles.
