UX Objectives

The Meal Planner should minimize cooking decisions by transforming kitchen intelligence into actionable meal recommendations.

UX Principles
Kitchen First

Recommendations originate from inventory.

Explainable Intelligence

Every recommendation includes reasoning.

Waste Reduction

Prioritize meals using ingredients nearing expiry.

User Control

Recommendations remain editable.

Calm Decision Making

Avoid overwhelming users with excessive meal choices.
