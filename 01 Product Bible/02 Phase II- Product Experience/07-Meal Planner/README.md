Overview

The Meal Planner Experience is PantryPal's AI-powered meal planning system that transforms kitchen intelligence into practical cooking decisions.

Unlike conventional recipe applications that begin with recipe discovery or keyword search, PantryPal begins with the user's current kitchen. Every recommendation is generated by analyzing available ingredients, inventory freshness, nutritional objectives, and kitchen readiness.

The Meal Planner helps users decide what to cook today, what to prepare next, and what ingredients may be required while minimizing food waste and maximizing the use of existing inventory.

Rather than functioning as a recipe library, the Meal Planner acts as an intelligent cooking assistant that continuously adapts to the evolving state of the user's kitchen.

Purpose

The Meal Planner exists to answer one fundamental question:

"What can I cook today with what I already have?"

Instead of requiring users to search through thousands of recipes, PantryPal intelligently recommends meals that best fit their kitchen.

Experience Goals

The Meal Planner aims to:

Generate AI-powered meal recommendations.
Maximize ingredient utilization.
Reduce food waste.
Support healthier eating habits.
Improve Kitchen Ready Score.
Simplify everyday cooking decisions.
Connect seamlessly with the Shopping Experience when ingredients are missing.

Out of Scope (v1.0)

The following capabilities are intentionally excluded from PantryPal v1.0:

Automatic grocery ordering
AI-generated recipes
Smart appliance control
Voice-guided cooking
Household collaborative meal planning
Meal delivery integration
Dynamic calorie coaching

These features are reserved for future versions.

Core Principles

The Meal Planner follows the PantryPal Platform Foundation and is guided by the following principles:

Kitchen First

Recommendations always begin with available ingredients.

Waste Reduction

Meals that utilize ingredients nearing expiry receive higher priority.

Explainable Intelligence

Every recommendation clearly explains why it appears.

Simplicity

The experience should reduce decision fatigue rather than increase it.

User Control

Recommendations are editable, searchable, and filterable.

PantryPal recommends.

The user decides.

Primary User Outcomes

After using the Meal Planner, users should:

Know what meals they can prepare immediately.
Understand why meals are recommended.
Use more of their available ingredients.
Reduce unnecessary grocery purchases.
Maintain a healthier and more organized kitchen.
