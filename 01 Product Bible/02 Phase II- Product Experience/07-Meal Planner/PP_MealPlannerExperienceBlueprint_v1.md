Purpose

The Meal Planner Experience Blueprint defines the structural layout, information hierarchy, interaction flow, and spatial organization of PantryPal's intelligent meal planning workspace.

Unlike traditional recipe applications that prioritize recipe discovery, PantryPal prioritizes kitchen intelligence, ensuring every meal recommendation is derived from the user's current inventory and cooking context.
