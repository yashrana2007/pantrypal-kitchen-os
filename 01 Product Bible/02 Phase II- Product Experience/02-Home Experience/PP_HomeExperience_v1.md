Chapter 2 — Home Experience

Project: Evergreen

Version: 1.0

Status: 🟡 In Design

2.1 Purpose

The Home Experience serves as the command center of PantryPal.

Rather than functioning as a dashboard filled with statistics, it provides users with a calm, intelligent overview of their kitchen.

Every element on the Home screen should answer one question:

"What does my kitchen need today?"

2.2 Experience Philosophy

The Home Experience should reduce thinking, not demand attention.

The screen should feel like walking into a well-organized kitchen where everything important is already prepared.

2.3 Experience Goals

The Home Experience should help users:

Understand today's kitchen status in seconds.
Know what requires attention.
Discover helpful AI suggestions.
Feel confident about meal planning.
Avoid information overload.

The goal is awareness, not analytics.

2.4 First Impression

Within the first five seconds, users should understand:

Is my kitchen ready today?
What should I do next?
Is anything expiring?
What can I cook?
Do I need groceries?

The interface should answer these questions without requiring users to search.

2.5 Home Experience Priorities

Information should always appear in the following order:

Level 1 — Immediate Status
Kitchen Ready Score (KRS)
Kitchen Maturity Index (KMI)
Critical alerts
Level 2 — Today's Actions
Expiring items
Shopping reminders
AI suggestions
Meal recommendations
Level 3 — Kitchen Overview
Pantry summary
Refrigerator summary
Freezer summary
Shopping status
Level 4 — Discovery
Kitchen Stories
Seasonal insights
Weekly achievements
Healthy habits

The most important information always appears first.

2.7 Daily Focus

Every day, PantryPal identifies one primary focus.

Examples:

Two vegetables should be used today.
Milk will expire tomorrow.
Pantry is ready for the week.
Great job! No urgent actions today.

Users should never feel overwhelmed by multiple competing priorities.

2.8 Living Kitchen Integration

The Living Kitchen illustration should reflect the current state of the user's kitchen.

Examples:

Healthy kitchen
Rainy day ambience
Morning sunlight
Seasonal decorations

The Home screen should feel alive without becoming distracting.

2.9 AI Companion

The AI should proactively assist without interrupting.

Examples:

"You can make vegetable fried rice with today's ingredients."
"Buying tomatoes this week will complete three planned meals."
"Your pantry organization has improved significantly."

The tone should always be encouraging.

2.10 Success Definition

The Home Experience succeeds when users can answer these questions within ten seconds:

How healthy is my kitchen?
What should I do today?
What can I cook?
Do I need groceries?

If users need to navigate elsewhere for these answers, the Home Experience should be improved.

2.11 Evergreen Principle

The Home Experience is not a dashboard. It is the morning conversation between PantryPal and the kitchen.
