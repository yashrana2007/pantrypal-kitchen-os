# Overview

The Home Experience is the primary entry point of PantryPal and serves as the **daily command center** for the user's kitchen.

Rather than acting as a traditional dashboard, the Home Experience functions as an intelligent daily briefing that helps users understand the current state of their kitchen within seconds.

The experience is designed around one guiding philosophy:

> **"The Home screen is where the kitchen talks back."**

---

# Objectives

The Home Experience should answer four questions within **10 seconds**.

- ✅ Is my kitchen ready today?
- ✅ What needs attention?
- ✅ What should I cook?
- ✅ Do I need groceries?

The experience minimizes cognitive load by surfacing only the most relevant information for the current day.

---

# Design Principles

The Home Experience follows the PantryPal Design System and Experience Principles.

### Core Principles

- Calm over clutter
- AI that explains itself
- One priority at a time
- Daily usefulness
- Kitchen-first thinking
- Progressive disclosure
- Consistent visual hierarchy

# Success Metrics

The Home Experience is considered successful when users can:

- Understand kitchen readiness in under **10 seconds**
- Identify today's highest priority immediately
- Trust AI recommendations through clear explanations
- Feel calm rather than overwhelmed
- Navigate naturally without unnecessary interactions
