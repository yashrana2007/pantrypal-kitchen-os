1. Purpose

This document records the reasoning behind the Home Experience design decisions.

Its purpose is to preserve product intent, ensure design consistency, and provide future designers and engineers with the context needed to evolve the Home Experience without compromising the PantryPal vision.

2. Home Experience Philosophy

The Home screen is not where users manage their kitchen. It is where the kitchen communicates with its owner.

The Home Experience functions as a daily briefing rather than a traditional dashboard.

Users should understand the current state of their kitchen within seconds without needing to navigate deeper into the application.

3. Why Kitchen Ready Score is the Hero

The Kitchen Ready Score (KRS) represents the kitchen's readiness for today.

Unlike other metrics, it changes frequently and immediately reflects the current condition of the kitchen.

Making KRS the largest visual element ensures users can instantly determine whether any action is required.

4. Why Kitchen Maturity Index is Secondary

Kitchen Maturity Index (KMI) measures long-term progress.

Unlike KRS, it evolves slowly over weeks and months.

Its purpose is to motivate continued engagement rather than drive immediate action.

Therefore, it is intentionally presented as a secondary component.

5. Why Expiring Items Shares the Secondary Row

Food expiry directly impacts food waste and meal planning.

Placing Expiring Items alongside KMI balances long-term growth with short-term urgency.

This pairing allows users to understand both their kitchen's evolution and today's risks at a glance.

6. Why There is Only One Today's Focus Card

Multiple reminders increase cognitive load.

PantryPal prioritizes a single actionable recommendation each day.

This approach reduces decision fatigue and encourages consistent progress.

7. Why AI Suggestions Explain Their Recommendations

Trust is a core principle of PantryPal.

Each AI recommendation includes a brief explanation, such as available ingredients or upcoming expiry dates.

Providing reasoning improves transparency and user confidence.

8. Why There is No Search on the Home Screen

Search is an intentional action, not a daily task.

The Home Experience focuses on proactive guidance rather than manual exploration.

Removing the search field keeps the interface uncluttered and reinforces the "Kitchen Briefing" concept.

9. Why the Living Kitchen is Not Displayed Here

The Living Kitchen is a dedicated immersive experience.

Keeping it separate preserves its emotional impact and prevents the Home screen from becoming visually crowded.

The Home Experience remains focused on clarity and actionable information.

10. Why Smart Add Contains Only Four Actions

The majority of pantry additions occur through:

Camera Scan
Receipt Scan
Voice Input
Manual Entry

Restricting Smart Add to these four methods maintains simplicity and supports the principle of reducing user effort.

11. Why Kitchen Story Appears at the End

Kitchen Stories are reflective rather than actionable.

They celebrate achievements and encourage long-term engagement.

Positioning them lower in the experience ensures critical tasks are completed first.

12. Visual Design Principles

The Home Experience follows the PantryPal Design System.

Key characteristics include:

Warm Cream backgrounds
Evergreen Green accents
Minimal shadows
Rounded cards
Generous whitespace
Calm visual hierarchy

The design intentionally avoids vibrant gradients and overly decorative elements.

13. Home Screen Contract

The following elements are mandatory and should remain consistent unless a future version of the Product Bible explicitly changes them:

Kitchen Ready Score as the primary hero.
Kitchen Maturity Index in the secondary row.
Expiring Items beside KMI.
One Today's Focus card.
Explainable AI Suggestions.
Four Smart Add shortcuts.
Five-item bottom navigation.
No search bar.
Living Kitchen excluded from the Home Experience.
14. Future Evolution

Potential enhancements include:

Adaptive layouts based on KMI.
Context-aware greetings.
AI Confidence indicators.
Seasonal visual themes.
Personalised Kitchen Stories.

These additions must preserve the existing information hierarchy.

15. Success Criteria

The Home Experience is successful when:

Users understand their kitchen's status within ten seconds.
Daily priorities are immediately clear.
AI recommendations are trusted.
Users feel informed rather than overwhelmed.
16. Evergreen Principle

A successful Home Experience should feel less like opening an application and more like receiving a thoughtful morning update from your kitchen.
