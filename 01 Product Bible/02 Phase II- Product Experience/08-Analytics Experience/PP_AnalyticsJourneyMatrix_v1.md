Purpose

The Journey Matrix maps every interaction between the user, PantryPal Intelligence, and interface components throughout the Analytics Experience.

| Review AI Recommendations | Understand improvements | Read AI explanations                                 | Generate personalized insights | Recommendation Cards | Confident       | Recommendations understood     |
