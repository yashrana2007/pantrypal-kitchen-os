System Responsibilities
| System                    | Responsibility                          |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Kitchen Intelligence Core | Coordinates analytics processing        |
| Analytics Engine          | Generates trends and insights           |
| Kitchen Ready Score       | Measures operational readiness          |

Workflow Principles
Historical data becomes meaningful insights.
AI explains every recommendation.
Analytics always encourages action.
Progress is celebrated.
