1. Purpose

The Analytics User Journey illustrates how users explore, understand, and act upon insights generated from their historical kitchen activity.

Unlike traditional dashboards that emphasize numbers, PantryPal Analytics emphasizes understanding and improvement.

2. Journey Objective

Help users recognize trends, evaluate kitchen performance, and take meaningful actions to improve long-term kitchen management.

3. User Persona
Primary Persona

Organized Home Manager

Goals

Improve kitchen efficiency
Reduce food waste
Build healthier habits
Monitor long-term progress
Make informed decisions
4. Entry Points

Users can access Analytics through:

Bottom Navigation
