Purpose

The Analytics Experience Blueprint defines the structural layout, information hierarchy, interaction flow, and intelligence presentation of PantryPal's Analytics workspace.

Rather than functioning as a reporting dashboard, Analytics presents meaningful insights that help users continuously improve their kitchen.
