1. Experience Overview

The Analytics Experience transforms historical kitchen activity into actionable intelligence that helps users improve their everyday kitchen management.

Instead of displaying isolated charts or metrics, PantryPal organizes insights into meaningful categories that explain the current state of the kitchen, identify long-term trends, and recommend practical improvements.

The Analytics Experience serves as PantryPal's reflection layer, helping users understand how their daily actions contribute to healthier, more organized kitchens.

2. Problem Statement

Traditional analytics dashboards often overwhelm users with statistics that provide little practical value.

Users frequently struggle to understand:

Whether their kitchen habits are improving.
Why food waste occurs.
How shopping behavior affects inventory.
Whether nutrition goals are being achieved.
What actions will create the greatest improvement.

As a result, analytics become passive reports instead of useful guidance.

3. Experience Vision

The Analytics Experience should feel like a personal kitchen coach that continuously interprets kitchen activity and highlights meaningful opportunities for improvement.

Instead of asking:

"What numbers do you want to see?"

PantryPal answers:

"Here's how your kitchen is improving and what you can do next."

4. Objectives

The Analytics Experience should:

Measure overall kitchen performance.

10. Design Principles
Insight First

Present conclusions before visualizations.

Explainable AI

Every recommendation should explain its reasoning.

Positive Reinforcement

Celebrate progress instead of emphasizing failures.

Simplicity

Keep analytics approachable through clean visual hierarchy.

Consistency

Follow PantryPal's Platform Foundation, Design System, Component Registry, and Interaction Principles.
