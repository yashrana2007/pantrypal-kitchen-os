1. Experience Overview

The Shopping Experience is an AI-assisted grocery planning experience that transforms inventory intelligence into actionable shopping decisions.

Rather than functioning as a traditional online grocery store, PantryPal continuously evaluates the kitchen and recommends purchases based on actual household needs.

Every recommendation is generated through PantryPal's intelligence layer and is designed to improve kitchen readiness while minimizing unnecessary purchases.

2. Problem Statement

Traditional grocery shopping relies heavily on memory and manual list creation.

Users often:

Forget essential ingredients.
Purchase duplicate items.
Miss expiring inventory.
Buy ingredients unrelated to upcoming meals.
Switch between multiple grocery applications to compare prices.

These challenges increase food waste, unnecessary spending, and decision fatigue.

3. Experience Vision

The Shopping Experience should feel like a personal kitchen assistant that quietly prepares an optimized grocery plan before the user even thinks about shopping.

Instead of asking:

"What do you want to buy?"

PantryPal asks:

"Based on your kitchen, here's what will keep you prepared."

4. Objectives

8. Core Capabilities
Smart Shopping List

Automatically prepares a shopping list based on kitchen intelligence.

AI Recommendations

Explains why each item should be purchased.

Examples:

Low stock
Expiring ingredient replacement
Upcoming meal requirement
Frequently purchased essential
Smart Cart

Allows users to review, edit, and prioritize recommended purchases before checkout.

Store Comparison

Compares supported grocery providers based on:

Product availability
Estimated delivery time
Overall cart price

The user selects the preferred option before checkout.

Checkout Redirection

PantryPal redirects users to the selected grocery provider to complete the purchase.

No payment processing occurs inside PantryPal.

10. Design Principles

The Shopping Experience follows these principles:

Intelligence First

Recommendations originate from AI-driven kitchen analysis.

Simplicity

Only necessary information is presented.

Transparency

Every recommendation includes a clear explanation.

Flexibility

Users can modify every recommendation.

Consistency

The experience follows PantryPal's Design System, PCR, and Interaction Patterns.
