Purpose

The Shopping Experience Blueprint defines the spatial structure, information hierarchy, user flow, and interaction zones of PantryPal's Shopping workspace.

Unlike conventional grocery apps that begin with products, PantryPal begins with kitchen intelligence, ensuring every shopping decision is grounded in the current state of the user's kitchen.
