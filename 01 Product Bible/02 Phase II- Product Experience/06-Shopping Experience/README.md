Overview

The Shopping Experience is PantryPal's intelligent grocery planning system. Unlike traditional grocery applications that begin with products or stores, PantryPal begins with the current state of the user's kitchen.

The Shopping Experience analyzes inventory, ingredient availability, upcoming cooking needs, expiry timelines, and user preferences to generate an AI-assisted shopping plan. Rather than acting as a grocery marketplace, PantryPal functions as an intelligence layer that helps users make informed purchasing decisions before redirecting them to their preferred grocery service.

This experience is designed to reduce food waste, avoid duplicate purchases, simplify grocery planning, and ensure the kitchen remains ready for everyday cooking.

Purpose

The Shopping Experience exists to answer one simple question:

"What does my kitchen actually need?"

Instead of requiring users to remember missing ingredients, PantryPal continuously evaluates kitchen readiness and proactively prepares a smart shopping plan.

Experience Goals

The Shopping Experience aims to:

Generate intelligent shopping recommendations.
Reduce duplicate purchases.
Prevent stock shortages.
Support meal planning.
Minimize food waste.
Simplify grocery management.
Compare supported grocery services before checkout.

Out of Scope (v1.0)

The following are intentionally excluded from PantryPal v1.0:

In-app payment processing
Grocery delivery management
Subscription ordering
Household collaborative shopping
Voice shopping assistant
Smart appliance ordering
Predictive purchasing

These remain candidates for future versions.

Core Principles

The Shopping Experience follows the PantryPal Platform Foundation and is built upon the following principles:

Kitchen First

Shopping decisions originate from the kitchen, not from a product catalog.

Intelligence Before Commerce

PantryPal recommends what should be purchased before presenting where it can be purchased.

Explainable Recommendations

Every shopping suggestion includes a clear reason.

Reduce Cognitive Load

Users review an AI-generated list instead of building one from scratch.

User Control

Recommendations remain editable at every stage.

PantryPal assists.

The user decides.

Primary User Outcomes

After using the Shopping Experience, users should:

Know what items require replenishment.
Understand why each recommendation was made.
Build an optimized shopping cart.
Compare supported grocery platforms.
Complete purchases with confidence.
