1. Purpose

The Shopping User Journey illustrates how users transition from identifying shopping needs to completing their grocery purchase using PantryPal's AI-assisted decision-making process.

Unlike traditional shopping applications, PantryPal begins with the current state of the kitchen rather than a product search.

2. Journey Objective

Enable users to efficiently purchase the right ingredients at the right time while minimizing waste, avoiding duplicate purchases, and maintaining kitchen readiness.

3. User Persona
Primary Persona

Busy Professional

Goals

Finish grocery planning quickly
Avoid forgetting ingredients
Reduce unnecessary purchases
Compare grocery services efficiently
4. Entry Points

Users can begin the Shopping Experience through:

Shopping Tab
Kitchen Ready Card
Low Inventory Alert
Meal Planner
Dashboard Shortcut
5. User Journey
Stage 1 — Discover Shopping Needs
User Goal

Understand what the kitchen requires.

User Actions
Opens Shopping
Reviews Shopping Overview
PantryPal Intelligence
Analyzes inventory
Detects low-stock items
Checks upcoming meals
Evaluates Kitchen Ready Score
Emotional State

Curious

Stage 2 — Review AI Recommendations
User Goal

Understand why items are recommended.

User Actions
Reviews recommendation cards
Reads AI explanations
Removes unnecessary items
PantryPal Intelligence

Generates recommendations using:

Inventory
KRS
Meal Planner
Context Engine
Emotional State

Confident

Stage 3 — Build Smart Cart
User Goal

Prepare shopping cart.

User Actions
Accept recommendations
Modify quantities
Add manual items
PantryPal Intelligence
Detects duplicates
Suggests complementary items
Updates shopping priorities
Emotional State

In Control

Stage 4 — Compare Stores
User Goal

Choose the best grocery provider.

User Actions
Views available stores
Compares total prices
Compares delivery estimates
PantryPal Intelligence

Aggregates:

Availability
Cart price
Delivery estimates
Emotional State

Satisfied

Stage 5 — Complete Shopping
User Goal

Finish grocery purchase.

User Actions
Chooses provider
Continues to checkout
PantryPal Intelligence

Prepares final cart for handoff.

Emotional State

Accomplished

6. Journey Summary
Kitchen Analysis
        ↓
Shopping Recommendations
        ↓
Review AI Suggestions
        ↓
Build Smart Cart
        ↓
Compare Grocery Stores
        ↓
Checkout
7. Experience Principles
Kitchen First
Intelligence Before Commerce
Explainable AI
User Always in Control
Calm Decision Making
Success Criteria

Users should:

Spend less time planning groceries.
Purchase only necessary items.
Improve kitchen readiness.
Reduce forgotten ingredients.
Complete shopping confidently.
