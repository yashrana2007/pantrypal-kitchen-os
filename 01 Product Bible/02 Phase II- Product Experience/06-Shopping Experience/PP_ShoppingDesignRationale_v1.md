Design Goal

Transform grocery shopping into an intelligent decision-making experience.

Why Kitchen First?

Traditional grocery apps:

Products → Cart → Checkout

PantryPal:

Kitchen → Intelligence → Shopping → Checkout

Why Recommendations?

Users shouldn't need to remember:

Milk
Rice
Eggs
Flour

PantryPal remembers.

Why Explainable AI?

Trust increases when users know why items appear.

Example:

Milk recommended because only one serving remains.

Why Manual Editing?

AI assists.

Humans decide.

Why Store Comparison?

Reduce app switching.

Increase transparency.

Improve purchasing decisions.

Expected Outcomes
Less food waste
Better planning
Faster shopping
Increased trust
