Purpose

The Shopping Journey Matrix maps user actions, emotions, AI intelligence, interface touchpoints, and system responses throughout the Shopping Experience.

| Journey Stage               | User Goal                       | User Actions                      | AI Intelligence                                      | UI Touchpoints                        | Emotional State | Success Criteria                 |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Discover Shopping Needs** | Understand kitchen requirements | Open Shopping Experience          | Analyze inventory, KRS, meal requirements            | Shopping Overview, Kitchen Ready Card | Curious         | Shopping list generated          |
| **Review Recommendations**  | Understand suggested purchases  | Read recommendation cards         | Explain recommendations using inventory intelligence | Recommendation Cards                  | Confident       | User understands recommendations |
| **Build Smart Cart**        | Prepare grocery cart            | Add/remove items, edit quantities | Detect duplicates, optimize cart                     | Smart Cart                            | In Control      | Optimized shopping cart created  |
| **Compare Stores**          | Select grocery provider         | Compare stores                    | Aggregate price, availability, delivery estimates    | Store Comparison Cards                | Confident       | Best provider selected           |
| **Checkout**                | Complete purchase               | Continue to provider              | Prepare checkout handoff                             | Checkout Summary                      | Accomplished    | Shopping completed               |
