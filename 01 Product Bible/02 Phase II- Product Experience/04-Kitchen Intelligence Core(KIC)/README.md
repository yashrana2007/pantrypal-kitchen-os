# Overview

The Kitchen Intelligence Core (KIC) is the central intelligence architecture of PantryPal.

It provides the shared reasoning, learning, and decision-making capabilities that power every intelligent experience within the Kitchen Operating System.

Unlike individual features, KIC is not directly visible to users. Instead, it continuously observes, understands, decides, and evolves alongside the kitchen to support better recommendations, automation, and user experiences.

Every AI-powered feature inside PantryPal references the Kitchen Intelligence Core.

---

# Purpose

The Kitchen Intelligence Core exists to:

- Transform raw kitchen data into meaningful intelligence.
- Maintain consistency across every AI feature.
- Separate user experiences from intelligence implementation.
- Enable future AI capabilities through a modular architecture.
- Provide a single source of truth for PantryPal's intelligence model.

# User Intelligence Indicators (UII)

These indicators help users understand the current state and long-term health of their kitchen.

Included:

- Kitchen Ready Score (KRS)
- Kitchen Maturity Index (KMI)

These metrics are user-facing and appear throughout PantryPal's experiences.

---

# System Intelligence Layer (SIL)

These metrics help PantryPal evaluate and improve its own intelligence.

Currently includes:

- AI Confidence Score (AICS)

Future system metrics may include:

- Recognition Accuracy
- Prediction Quality
- Learning Progress
- Vision Performance
- Speech Recognition Quality

These metrics primarily support AI behavior, diagnostics, and engineering.
