1. Purpose

The Kitchen Intelligence Core (KIC) is the central intelligence layer of PantryPal.

It is responsible for transforming raw kitchen data into meaningful decisions, recommendations, and experiences.

Unlike individual features, KIC is not a user-facing component. It is the shared intelligence system that powers every major experience within PantryPal.

Every AI capability in PantryPal is built upon the Kitchen Intelligence Core.

2. Vision

To create an adaptive intelligence system that continuously observes, understands, assists, and evolves alongside every kitchen.

The Kitchen Intelligence Core should reduce effort, increase confidence, and make every interaction with PantryPal more valuable over time.

3. Philosophy

"A smart kitchen is not one that knows everything. It is one that learns continuously while keeping the user in control."

Artificial Intelligence exists to support decision-making rather than replace it.

Users should always understand why PantryPal makes recommendations and should always have the ability to confirm or modify AI decisions.

4. Objectives

The Kitchen Intelligence Core has four primary objectives.

Observe

Collect meaningful kitchen information with minimal user effort.

Understand

Interpret collected information into structured kitchen knowledge.

Decide

Generate helpful recommendations, insights, and automation.

Evolve

Improve continuously through user interaction and learning.

8. Core Intelligence Metrics

The Kitchen Intelligence Core exposes three primary intelligence metrics.

Kitchen Ready Score (KRS)

Measures the operational readiness of the kitchen.

Updates frequently.

Focuses on the present.

Kitchen Maturity Index (KMI)

Measures long-term kitchen growth.

Updates gradually.

Focuses on progress.

AI Confidence Score (AICS)

Measures PantryPal's confidence in its understanding of the kitchen.

Determines when AI should ask for confirmation.

Focuses on trust.

9. Intelligence Principles

Every AI feature inside PantryPal must follow these principles.

AI assists before asking.
Users always remain in control.
Explain every important recommendation.
Learn continuously.
Minimize manual work.
Prefer transparency over automation.
Respect uncertainty.
Never overwhelm users.
Build trust through consistency.
