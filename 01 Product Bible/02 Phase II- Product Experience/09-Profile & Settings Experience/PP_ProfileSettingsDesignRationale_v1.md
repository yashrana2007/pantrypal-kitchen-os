Design Goal

Create a settings experience that feels calm, trustworthy, and effortless to use.

Why Organized Sections?

Users should locate settings quickly without searching through long lists.

Grouping settings by purpose improves discoverability and reduces cognitive load.

Why Kitchen Profile?

Kitchen preferences directly influence recommendations across Smart Add, Shopping, Meal Planner, and Analytics.

Centralizing these settings ensures consistent personalization.

Why Privacy First?

Trust is essential for an AI-powered Kitchen Operating System.

Privacy controls should be easy to understand and never hidden behind complex menus.

Why Immediate Feedback?

Users should instantly know when preferences have been successfully updated.

This builds confidence and reduces uncertainty.

Why Minimal Complexity?

Most users rarely visit settings.

The interface should remain clean, approachable, and task-focused.

Expected Outcomes
Faster profile setup.
Better personalization.
Increased user trust.
Higher notification relevance.
Consistent experience across PantryPal.
