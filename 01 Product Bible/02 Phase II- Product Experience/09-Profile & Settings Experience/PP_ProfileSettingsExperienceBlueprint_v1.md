Purpose

The Profile & Settings Experience Blueprint defines the structural layout, information hierarchy, personalization flow, and interaction model of PantryPal's Profile & Settings workspace.

Unlike operational experiences, Profile & Settings acts as the personalization and configuration center of the Kitchen Operating System, enabling users to manage their identity, preferences, privacy, and application behavior.
