Overview

The Profile & Settings Experience serves as PantryPal's personalization and account management hub. It enables users to customize preferences, manage their profile, configure kitchen settings, review privacy options, and control the overall behavior of PantryPal.

Unlike the operational experiences—such as Smart Add, Living Kitchen, Shopping, Meal Planner, and Analytics—Profile & Settings focuses on personalization, trust, and system configuration. It ensures that PantryPal adapts to the user's preferences while maintaining transparency, privacy, and consistency.

The experience is intentionally designed to remain simple and unobtrusive, allowing users to configure the platform without disrupting their everyday kitchen workflows.

Purpose

The Profile & Settings Experience exists to answer one fundamental question:

"How does PantryPal adapt to me?"

Rather than exposing complex configuration options, PantryPal provides a clear, organized, and approachable settings experience that supports personalization without unnecessary complexity.

Experience Goals

The Profile & Settings Experience aims to:

Personalize the PantryPal experience.
Manage user account information.
Configure kitchen preferences.
Control notifications.
Manage privacy and security settings.
Customize application behavior.
Provide access to help and support resources.
Experience Scope (v1.0)

The Profile & Settings Experience includes:

User Profile
Kitchen Profile
Dietary Preferences
Notification Settings
Appearance Preferences
Privacy & Security
Connected Services
Help & Support
About PantryPal
Account Management
Out of Scope (v1.0)

The following capabilities are intentionally excluded:

Multi-user household management
Smart appliance configuration
Voice assistant settings
Developer options
Third-party automation rules
Advanced AI customization
Multi-home management

These remain candidates for future versions.

Core Principles

The Profile & Settings Experience follows the PantryPal Platform Foundation and is built upon the following principles.

Simplicity

Settings should be easy to find, understand, and modify.

Transparency

Users should always understand what a setting controls.

Privacy First

Personal information and permissions should be presented clearly and securely.

Personalization

PantryPal should adapt to users without requiring extensive configuration.

Consistency

Settings should follow the same design language as every other PantryPal experience.

Primary User Outcomes

After using Profile & Settings, users should:

Feel confident that PantryPal reflects their preferences.
Understand their account and privacy settings.
Configure notifications and kitchen preferences.
Access support when needed.
Trust the platform with their personal information.
