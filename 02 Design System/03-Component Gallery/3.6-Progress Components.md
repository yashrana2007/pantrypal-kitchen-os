3.6 Progress Components

Project: Evergreen

Version: 1.0 Alpha

Status: 🟡 In Review

3.6.1 Purpose

Progress Components visualize the health, readiness, and completion status of the user's kitchen and nutrition journey.

Rather than displaying percentages alone, PantryPal uses meaningful progress indicators that encourage action while remaining calm and approachable.

Progress indicators should motivate users, not pressure them.

3.6.2 Progress Philosophy

Every progress indicator should answer one question.

"How am I doing right now?"

The goal is understanding rather than perfection.

3.6.4 Kitchen Ready Ring ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Purpose

The Kitchen Ready Ring is PantryPal's signature progress component.

It represents the overall readiness of the user's kitchen.

The score combines multiple factors including:

Pantry availability
Freshness
Expiry management
Nutrition readiness
Meal preparedness

This component should appear prominently on the Home Dashboard.

Displays
Percentage Score
Kitchen Status
Supporting message

Example:

92% Kitchen Ready

"Everything looks great for the week."

Rules
One Kitchen Ready Ring per dashboard.
Always the largest progress component.
Smooth animated transitions.
Never display unnecessary technical details.

3.6.15 Color Usage

Progress components follow PantryPal semantic colors.

🟢 Green

Healthy progress.

🟡 Amber

Needs attention.

🔴 Red

Critical attention.

🔵 Blue

Informational progress.

Progress colors should communicate status without overwhelming users.

3.6.16 Motion

Progress animations should:

Fill naturally.
Never exceed one second.
Avoid abrupt movement.
Reinforce positive progress.
3.6.17 Accessibility

Every progress component must include:

Numeric value
Text description
High contrast
Screen reader support

Progress should never rely solely on color.

3.6.18 Living Progress

Progress indicators subtly respond to PantryPal's Living Environment.

Examples:

Morning

Warm ambient glow.

Night

Softer illumination.

Healthy Kitchen

Gentle green pulse.

Goal Achieved

Small leaf growth animation.

These enhancements should remain subtle.

3.6.19 Do
Keep progress simple.
Explain what is being measured.
Encourage users.
Celebrate milestones modestly.
3.6.20 Don't
Use aggressive colors unnecessarily.
Shame users for low progress.
Animate continuously.
Display excessive numerical complexity.
3.6.21 Why This Component Family Matters

Progress Components transform raw data into meaningful insight.

They help users understand the current state of their kitchen and health at a glance, encouraging steady improvement without creating pressure.

These components play a central role in PantryPal's calm, supportive experience.

🌿 Evergreen Principle

Progress should inspire confidence, not perfection.
