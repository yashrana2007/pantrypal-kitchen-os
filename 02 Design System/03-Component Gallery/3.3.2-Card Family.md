3.3.1 Overview

The PantryPal Card Gallery defines every reusable card component used throughout the application.

Cards are the primary building blocks of PantryPal and are designed to present information in a calm, organized, and highly scannable manner.

Every card represents one meaningful concept and follows the PantryPal Card Architecture.

The gallery ensures visual consistency across all screens while reducing design complexity and improving scalability.

3.3.2 Card Philosophy

PantryPal follows the principle:

One Card. One Story.

Every card should answer only one primary question.

Examples include:

Is my kitchen ready?
What should I cook today?
Which ingredient needs attention?
How healthy is my pantry?
Where can I save money?

Cards should never become information dashboards.

Complex information should be divided into multiple focused cards.

3.3.3 Card Categories

The PantryPal Card Gallery is divided into three categories.

Signature Cards

These cards define PantryPal's identity.

Kitchen Ready Hero Card
Kitchen Zone Card
Ingredient Card
Smart Suggestion Card
Smart Checkout Card

These cards appear throughout the application and should remain visually distinctive.

Everyday Cards

Used for routine interactions.

Meal Card
Analytics Card
Planner Card
Grocery Item Card
Statistics Card

These provide users with daily information while maintaining simplicity.

Supporting Cards

Used for guidance and secondary information.

Achievement Card
Alert Card
Empty State Card
Timeline Card

Supporting cards should remain visually lightweight and never compete with primary content.

3.3.4 Kitchen Ready Hero Card
Purpose

The Kitchen Ready Hero Card represents the overall health and readiness of the user's kitchen.

It serves as the primary visual focus of the Home Dashboard.

Displays
Kitchen Ready Score
Overall Status
Weekly Summary
Primary Recommendation
Quick Action
Rules
Only one Hero Card per screen.
Always placed at the top of the Dashboard.
Never used on secondary screens.

3.3.19 Living Card Behaviour

Selected cards support adaptive environmental behaviour.

These cards respond to:

Morning
Afternoon
Evening
Night
Weather
Seasons
Festivals
Kitchen Health

The layout remains unchanged.

Only the atmosphere evolves.

Living behaviour is reserved for signature cards to preserve its impact.

3.3.20 Accessibility

All cards must comply with PantryPal accessibility standards.

Requirements include:

Minimum 48 × 48 px interactive targets
Clear visual hierarchy
Readable typography
Sufficient contrast
Screen reader compatibility
