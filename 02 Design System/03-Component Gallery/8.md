3.8.1 Purpose

The Illustration Library gives PantryPal warmth, personality, and emotional connection.

Illustrations should support communication, reduce cognitive load, and make everyday kitchen tasks feel friendly rather than transactional.

Illustrations should never exist purely for decoration.

Every illustration should have a purpose.

3.8.2 Illustration Philosophy

Illustrations should make the kitchen feel alive, not the interface feel busy.

3.8.3 Illustration Style

PantryPal illustrations follow a consistent visual language:

Soft rounded geometry
Minimal details
Organic shapes
Friendly proportions
Calm expressions
Flat illustration style
Light depth through subtle shadows

Avoid realism.

Avoid cartoon exaggeration.

3.8.4 Color Language

Illustrations use PantryPal Design Tokens.

Primary:

Supporting:

🍃 Fresh Green

Background:

🤍 Warm Cream

Accent:

🌾 Soft Beige

Status colors only when required.

3.8.6 Illustration Principles

Every illustration should:

Feel optimistic
Be easy to understand
Reinforce PantryPal's identity
Scale well
Remain timeless
3.8.7 Accessibility

Illustrations should never be the only way to communicate information.

They always accompany text and actions.

3.8.8 Emotional Tone

Illustrations should communicate:

Calm
Encouragement
Freshness
Growth
Home
