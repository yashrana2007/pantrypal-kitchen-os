3.9.1 Purpose

Motion helps users understand change, maintain context, and interact naturally with PantryPal.

Animations should communicate state, guide attention, and reinforce the Living Kitchen philosophy.

Motion should never exist purely for decoration.

3.9.2 Motion Philosophy

Motion should feel like nature—not machinery.

Just as leaves sway gently and sunlight changes gradually, PantryPal's interface should move with calm, subtle transitions.

3.9.3 Motion Principles

Every animation should be:

Calm
Purposeful
Organic
Responsive
Minimal
Consistent

3.9.7 Signature Animations
🌿 Kitchen Ready Ring

Ring fills smoothly.

Leaf grows slightly.

Status fades in.

📷 Smart Add

Camera gently pulses.

Scan line moves softly.

Success confirmation appears.

🍽 Smart Meal

Meal image scales in.

Nutrition cards appear one by one.

🧠 Kitchen AI

Three small nodes orbit gently around the leaf.

No spinning robots.

No glowing effects.

🛒 Smart Checkout

Shopping cart rolls slightly.

Checkmark grows naturally.

3.9.9 Loading

Prefer:

Skeleton loading
Progress indicators
Gentle shimmer

Avoid:

Infinite spinners where possible
3.9.10 Accessibility

Animations must respect reduced-motion preferences.

Users can disable decorative animations.

Essential feedback animations remain functional.

3.9.11 Living Motion

This is unique to PantryPal.

Examples:

Morning

Soft warm lighting.

Rain

Tiny window droplets.

Healthy kitchen

Plants sway gently.

Kitchen Ready

Leaves grow over time.

These motions should remain subtle and never distract from the user's task.

3.9.12 Do
Animate with purpose.
Keep timing consistent.
Reinforce spatial relationships.
Celebrate achievements calmly.
3.9.13 Don't
Overuse animation.
Use flashy effects.
Delay user actions.
Distract from content.
🌿 Evergreen Principle

Motion should help users feel that their kitchen is alive—not that their phone is animated.
