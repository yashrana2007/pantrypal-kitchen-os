Executive Statement

Project Evergreen represents the complete product definition of PantryPal, an AI-powered Kitchen Operating System designed to transform kitchen management into an intelligent, contextual, and human-centered experience.

This Master Edition serves as the official entry point to the PantryPal Knowledge System and consolidates every strategic, product, design, and governance document produced during the v1.0 documentation phase.

It is the authoritative reference for product management, design, engineering, quality assurance, and future contributors.

Product Vision

To build the world's most intelligent, human-centered Kitchen Operating System that transforms every kitchen into a living, connected, and intelligent environment.

PantryPal combines inventory intelligence, shopping assistance, meal planning, analytics, and personalization into a unified ecosystem that helps users make smarter everyday kitchen decisions.

Documentation Mission

Project Evergreen exists to ensure that PantryPal is designed, documented, and implemented with clarity, consistency, and long-term scalability.

Every document contributes to a single, governed knowledge system where product intent is preserved from concept through production.
